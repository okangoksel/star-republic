# STAR REPUBLIC — Original Design Direction

Star Republic is not a clone of Minecraft, Stardew Valley, Terraria or another existing game.
Those games can inspire broad genres, but Star Republic's systems, terminology, progression and world identity are original.

## Core identity
A frontier survival RPG where the player starts with nothing and gradually builds a settlement around a strange resource phenomenon called the **Astral Bloom**.

## Original progression
Empty hands → gather natural fibers/branches → improvise primitive tools → establish a workbench → unlock settlement technology → investigate Astral Bloom → specialize → open dangerous regions.

The player does not receive a complete starter kit.

## Original systems to develop
- **Improvisation:** primitive tools are assembled from discovered materials rather than handed to the player.
- **Resonance:** certain materials have a hidden resonance value. Combining compatible materials changes tool behavior.
- **Fieldcraft:** temporary camp structures can be placed in the wilderness and later become permanent outposts.
- **Echo Events:** places remember what happened there; repeated activity can change resource and enemy behavior.
- **Astral Bloom:** a late-introduced world phenomenon that slowly connects farming, mining, crafting and exploration.
- **Settlement evolution:** the player's base grows according to what they actually build and use.
- **Discovery recipes:** some recipes are learned by experimentation, exploration or NPC knowledge rather than a static recipe list.

## Design rule
Do not copy names, art, maps, UI layouts, progression beats, lore, characters or distinctive mechanics from existing games.
Use familiar genre foundations only where they are generic and necessary for playability.
